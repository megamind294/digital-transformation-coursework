# Reference Categories for Further Study

This repository intentionally does **not** invent a bibliography for the unrecovered original assignments. When extending the literature review, use verifiable academic and professional sources from categories such as:

- peer-reviewed research on digital transformation definitions and organizational capabilities;
- technology-management and information-systems journals;
- research on digital business models and customer experience;
- organizational change and technology-adoption literature;
- cybersecurity, privacy, data governance, and AI-governance standards;
- official documentation for technologies discussed in a case study;
- recognized enterprise-architecture and process-modelling references.

For academic work, record complete author, title, publication, year, DOI/URL, and access details according to the citation style required by the course. Only cite a source after actually reading and verifying it.
