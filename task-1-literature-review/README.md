# Task 1 — Literature Review Reconstruction

Recovered course notes identify Task 1 as a **Digital Transformation literature-review assignment** in the English Master's study track.

The original completed document is not available in the accessible file library. This folder therefore preserves the assignment theme through a new portfolio-oriented literature-review framework rather than pretending to reproduce the submitted paper.

## Review question

**How does digital transformation change organizational capabilities, operating models, and customer value, and what conditions influence whether transformation initiatives succeed?**

## Suggested review structure

1. Define digital transformation and distinguish it from digitization and digitalization.
2. Examine common business and technology drivers.
3. Review enabling technologies such as cloud platforms, analytics, AI, automation, APIs, and modern data architectures.
4. Analyze organizational capabilities: leadership, culture, skills, governance, agile delivery, and change management.
5. Consider customer-experience and operating-model impacts.
6. Evaluate risk areas including cybersecurity, privacy, legacy integration, vendor dependence, and transformation fatigue.
7. Discuss how outcomes can be measured through operational, customer, financial, and capability metrics.
8. Synthesize findings and identify tensions or research gaps.

See `review-notes.md` for concise study notes organized around these themes.
